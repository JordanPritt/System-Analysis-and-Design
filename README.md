# System Analysis and Design

> Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?

The client is a start-up focusing on a driving instruction niche to help prepare individuals for driving tests and to be better drivers. They wanted me to design a cloud-based system to centralize their in-house processes, share resources with students, and manage scheduling of their services for students to proactively participate in.

> What did you do particularly well?

I believe I captured the needs of the start-up well. I identified what system components were needed to design a system that meets the needs of their proposals without over complicating the design beyond what is needed to achieve their goals with.

> If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

The one part I would revise would be the various UML diagrams by adding further details and structure to them. Having high defined documentation like these diagrams helps reduce the burden on implementation assumptions, so providing the clearest and most detailed pieces up front would be preferrable.

> How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?

I interpreted the user’s needs by viewing the system as a use would so I could map components to the actual use cases for better design and analysis. This is important because users are who actually use the system, so ensuring that they are able to use it and that the system meets the needs requested are paramount to success.

> How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?

I approach software design by defining what the end-goal is of the software that I am building is going to be designed to do, then listing the requirements needed to achieve that goal. From there I build a backlog of tasks or stories I break out from the requirements and goals so I can tackle the development incrementally and completely. Going forward I will try to add more diagrams and system design documentation as well for easier reference, as they help visualize systems and their components very well, which is invaluable when the code has not been touched in months or longer and needs to be revisited.
